CRGT Sample Workstation, offline version (v28)

What this is
  The same app as the Claude artifact, packed to live on your iPad's home screen and run with no connection:
  fonts are inside it, and after the first visit everything is kept on the iPad by a service worker.
  Numbers use the iPad's own monospace (SF Mono) instead of JetBrains Mono.

Put it online once (any HTTPS static host; GitHub Pages is free)
  1. Make a new repository on github.com (public), e.g. "crgt".
  2. Upload these files to it as they are: index.html, sw.js, manifest.webmanifest, icon-180.png, icon-192.png, icon-512.png.
  3. Settings > Pages > Build and deployment: "Deploy from a branch", branch main, folder / (root). Save.
  4. After a minute it is at https://YOUR-NAME.github.io/crgt/

Install on the iPad
  5. Open that address in Safari, tap Share > Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  6. Open CRGT from the home screen once while online and wait a few seconds (it saves itself).
  7. From then on it opens and plays in airplane mode.

Your sessions
  The home-screen app keeps its own sessions, separate from the Claude artifact. To bring one over: in the artifact,
  SESSION > export the session (.zip); in the home-screen app, SESSION > load that .zip. Takes and session exports
  from the home-screen app go through the share sheet: tap SAVE, then Save to Files.

Updating
  Replace the files in the repository with a newer build. The app picks it up the next time it is opened online,
  and uses it from the launch after.
